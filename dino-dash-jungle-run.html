Privacy Policy — Dino Dash: Jungle Run

Last updated: 30 August 2026

App: Dino Dash: Jungle Run (com.innovix.dinodashjungle)

Developer: Innovix

Contact: innovixdigitalsolutions@gmail.com

Summary

Dino Dash: Jungle Run is a game made for children. It does not ask for your name, your email or any account. The game itself works completely offline. It does not use the advertising identifier, and the ads it shows are never personalised.

What stays on your device only

The following is stored locally with Android's standard app preferences and is never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

  • Your high score, in metres
  • The total number of coins you have collected across all runs
  • Which dino skin you have equipped, and which ones you have unlocked
  • Your sound on/off setting
  • Counters that control how often ads may appear

What is collected and shared

Ads are served by Google AdMob, a Google Play Families self-certified ads SDK. To choose an advertisement it receives the following. All of it is sent encrypted (HTTPS).

Data typeWhy
IP address, from which an approximate location such as the country is estimatedto choose an ad that is valid in your region
App interactions — that an ad slot was requested, shown or closedto count and place ads
Diagnostics and crash information from the ads SDKto keep the SDK working

AdMob's own handling of this data is described in Google's Privacy Policy and the AdMob partner list.

The Android Advertising ID (AAID) is not collected and not shared. The app does not hold the advertising ID permission, and every ad request is tagged for child-directed treatment, so the advertising SDK does not transmit that identifier.

What is never collected

Name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera, precise location, or any account information. The app has no login, no chat, no user-generated content and no in-app purchases.

Dino skins are unlocked with coins collected while playing. Nothing in the game is sold and there is no in-game currency to buy.

Children

This app is made for children and follows the Google Play Families Policy.

  • No advertising identifier. The app does not request the Android advertising ID permission and does not transmit the AAID.
  • No personalised ads. Ads are never based on browsing behaviour or interests, and are never used for remarketing.
  • Ad content is rated G, so it stays suitable for children.
  • No other persistent identifiers — no SIM serial, build serial, BSSID, MAC, SSID, IMEI or IMSI.
  • No location permission and no precise location.
  • No purchases, no chat, no links out of the game. Coins are earned by playing and cannot be bought.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included. If you believe a child has given us personal information, write to the address below and we will delete it.

The Google advertising ID permission (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) is explicitly removed from this app, so the advertising identifier is not available to it.

The app asks for no camera, microphone, contacts, storage or location permission. It is fully playable offline: with no network connection the game runs normally and simply shows no advertisements.
