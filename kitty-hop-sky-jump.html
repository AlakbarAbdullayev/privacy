Privacy Policy — Kitty Hop: Sky Jump

Last updated: 30 August 2026

Innovix ("we") operates the mobile application Kitty Hop: Sky Jump

(package com.innovix.kittyhopsky, "the App").

Information we collect

The App does not require registration and does not collect names, email addresses or phone numbers.

The App displays advertisements through Google AdMob, a Google Play Families self-certified ads SDK. To select an advertisement, AdMob receives the IP address of the device and general, non-personal usage data. It does not receive the Android Advertising ID (AAID). Google's handling of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

We do not collect, transmit or store any other data about you.

Children

This app is made for children and follows the Google Play Families Policy.

  • No advertising identifier. The app does not request the Android advertising ID permission and does not transmit the AAID.
  • No personalised ads. Ads are never based on browsing behaviour or interests, and are never used for remarketing.
  • Ad content is rated G, so it stays suitable for children.
  • No other persistent identifiers — no SIM serial, build serial, BSSID, MAC, SSID, IMEI or IMSI.
  • No location permission and no precise location.
  • No purchases, no chat, no links out of the game.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included. If you believe a child has given us personal information, write to the address below and we will delete it.

Data storage

Your best height, collected stars, unlocked and selected cat, sound setting and

the counters that control how often an advertisement is shown are stored

locally on your device only, using Android's standard app preferences. They

are removed when you uninstall the App. We never upload them.

INTERNETRequired by Google AdMob to load advertisements

The Google advertising ID permission (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) is explicitly removed from this app, so the advertising identifier is not available to it.

The App is fully playable offline. If there is no network connection, the game

runs normally and simply shows no advertisements.
