Privacy Policy — Kitty Hop: Sky Jump

Last updated: 25 August 2026

Innovix ("we") operates the mobile application Kitty Hop: Sky Jump

(package com.innovix.kittyhopsky, "the App").

Information we collect

The App does not require registration and does not collect names, email

addresses or phone numbers.

The App displays advertisements through Google AdMob, which may collect

device identifiers (advertising ID), IP address and general usage data in order

to serve ads. Google's handling of this data is described at

<https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads>.

We do not collect, transmit or store any other data about you.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. Advertising is configured with a

maximum content rating of G, so ad content stays suitable for general

audiences.

Data storage

Your best height, collected stars, unlocked and selected cat, sound setting and

the counters that control how often an advertisement is shown are stored

locally on your device only, using Android's standard app preferences. They

are removed when you uninstall the App. We never upload them.
