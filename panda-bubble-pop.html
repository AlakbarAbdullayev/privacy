Summary

Panda Bubble Pop does not ask for your name, your email or any account. The

game itself works completely offline. The only data that leaves your device

is the advertising identifier used by Google AdMob to show ads.

What stays on your device only

The following is stored locally with Android's standard app preferences and

is never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

  • Which levels you have unlocked and how many stars you earned on each
  • Your hint balance and daily bonus streak
  • An unfinished level, so closing the app mid-level does not lose progress
  • Your sound on/off setting
  • Counters that control how often ads may appear

You can reset or delete this identifier at any time:

Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID.

What is never collected

Name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, microphone,

camera, precise location, or any account information. The app has no login,

no chat, no user-generated content and no in-app purchases.

Age

This app is not directed to children. Its target audience is 13 and

older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
