Privacy Policy — Panda Bubble Pop

Last updated: 30 August 2026

App: Panda Bubble Pop (com.innovix.pandabubblepop)

Summary

Panda Bubble Pop is a game made for children. It does not ask for your name, your email or any account. The game itself works completely offline. It does not use the advertising identifier, and the ads it shows are never personalised.

What stays on your device only

The following is stored locally with Android's standard app preferences and

is never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

  • Which levels you have unlocked and how many stars you earned on each
  • Your hint balance and daily bonus streak
  • An unfinished level, so closing the app mid-level does not lose progress
  • Your sound on/off setting
  • Counters that control how often ads may appear

What is collected and shared

Ads are served by Google AdMob, a Google Play Families self-certified ads SDK. To choose an advertisement it receives the following, all encrypted (HTTPS).

DataWhy
IP address, from which an approximate location such as the country is estimatedto choose an ad that is valid in your region
App interactions — that an ad slot was requested, shown or closedto count and place ads
Diagnostics and crash information from the ads SDKto keep the SDK working

AdMob's own handling of this data is described in Google's Privacy Policy and the AdMob partner list.

The Android Advertising ID (AAID) is not collected and not shared. The app does not hold the advertising ID permission, and every ad request is tagged for child-directed treatment.

What is never collected

Name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, microphone,

camera, precise location, or any account information. The app has no login,

no chat, no user-generated content and no in-app purchases.

Children

This app is made for children and follows the Google Play Families Policy.

  • No advertising identifier. The app does not request the Android advertising ID permission and does not transmit the AAID.
  • No personalised ads. Ads are never based on browsing behaviour or interests, and are never used for remarketing.
  • Ad content is rated G, so it stays suitable for children.
  • No other persistent identifiers — no SIM serial, build serial, BSSID, MAC, SSID, IMEI or IMSI.
  • No location permission and no precise location.
  • No purchases, no chat, no links out of the game.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included. If you believe a child has given us personal information, write to the address below and we will delete it.
