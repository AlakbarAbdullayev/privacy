Privacy Policy — Tables Arcade

Last updated: 07 November 2026

App: Tables Arcade (com.ailovedigital.tablesarcade)

Developer: AI LOVE DIGITAL, MMC

Contact: info@ailovedigital.com

Summary

Tables Arcade is a game made for children (ages 6–10) that helps them practise the multiplication and division tables. It asks for no name, e-mail or account. The game works completely offline. It does not use the advertising identifier, and the ads it shows are never personalised. There is no analytics or tracking software in the app.

What stays on your device only

Stored locally in the app's own storage and never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

  • Up to three player profiles: a first name or nickname the grown-up may type (optional), a hero picture, settings
  • Which facts of the tables each player knows, stars, stickers, levels, play time per day (to enforce the daily play limit)
  • Grown-up settings (tables range, race time, daily limit, sound)
  • Counters that control how rarely an ad may appear (install time, number of sessions, time of the last ad)
  • Whether ads were removed (a copy of what the store says)

What is collected and shared

The app shows a small banner on the map and result screens and, rarely, one short full-screen ad at the end of a finished round (at most once per session, never during play, never in the first day, never in the parent area or the store screen). There are no rewarded ads. Ads are served by Google AdMob, a Google Play Families self-certified ads SDK. No ad-mediation partners are bundled.

To choose an advertisement AdMob receives the following, all encrypted (HTTPS).

DataWhy
IP address, from which an approximate location such as the country is estimatedto choose an ad that is valid in the region
App interactions — that an ad slot was requested, shown or closedto count and place ads
Diagnostics and crash information from the ads SDKto keep the SDK working

AdMob's own handling of this data is described in Google's Privacy Policy and the AdMob partner list.

The Android Advertising ID (AAID) is not collected and not shared. The Google advertising ID permission (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) is explicitly removed from this app's merged manifest, and every ad request is tagged for child-directed treatment with the maximum content rating G.

Remove-ads purchase

A grown-up can pay once to remove all ads. The purchase is handled entirely by Google Play; the app never sees payment details, name or address — it only learns whether the purchase exists. The purchase sits behind a parental gate in the grown-ups' corner; children do not see a shop. All content is free with or without the purchase.

What is never collected

Name, e-mail address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera, precise location, or any account information. No login, chat or user-generated content. No analytics, no crash-reporting service, no social login.

Children

This app is made for children and follows the Google Play Families Policy.

  • No advertising identifier. The app does not hold the AD_ID advertising ID permission and does not transmit the AAID.
  • No personalised ads. No interest-based ads, no remarketing.
  • Ad content is rated G, so it stays suitable for children.
  • No other persistent identifiers and no location permission.
  • Links that leave the app and the purchase are behind a parental gate (three numbers written in words, then hold a button).

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included. If you believe a child has given us personal information, write to the address below and we will delete it.
